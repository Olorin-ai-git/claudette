## 2. The "why us" section (three pillars)

**Take the wheel**
When Claude hits a wall — an interactive rebase, a stuck service, a one-off
command — you don't wait until you're back at your laptop. One tap drops you into
the live shell. Run it. Hand control back. Claude picks up where you left off.

**Zero abstraction**
It's your Mac, your tmux, your prompt. Not a chat box pretending to be a terminal.
Full PTY, extended keyboard, real scrollback.

**Your machine, your keys**
SSH straight to your Mac — or over the Olorin Relay from anywhere. No middle-man
cloud running your code. Ed25519 keys generated on-device. Open source, top to
bottom.

## 3. Feature callouts (short, scannable)

- **Real shell, in the loop** — the only Claude client where a human can drive the
  agent's own session.
- **Structured + raw, one tap apart** — watch Claude's tool use as a clean
  conversation, flip to the terminal when you need to.
- **Multi-tab tmux** — sessions survive backgrounding and reconnect on their own.
- **Context & cost gauge** — live token usage and spend, with threshold warnings.
- **Agent visualizer** — watch the subagent tree work in real time.
- **Voice in, vocal summaries out** — dictate prompts; hear a summary when a task
  finishes.
- **SFTP, Bonjour, Wake-on-LAN** — it's a real workstation, not just a chat client.

## Copy notes

- **Lead with "take the wheel" everywhere.** It's the one durable differentiator;
  every other line is supporting cast.
- **Don't over-claim privacy as the headline** — it's a trust reinforcer (pillar 3),
  not the wedge. Leading with it invites "but I still pay for Claude anyway."
- **Name the rivals only implicitly** ("every other Claude app") — punchier and
  avoids a feature-checklist war Anthropic can win.
- **Fix native push before launch** so the listing doesn't draw a "no notifications"
  comparison in reviews.
